Privacy Policy — JSON Visualizer

Last updated: September 25, 2026

JSON Visualizer is a client-side Chrome extension that reformats JSON responses already loaded in your browser tab.

Data collection

We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share any data. There are no servers, no analytics, no accounts, and no tracking of any kind.

  • All parsing and rendering happens entirely inside your browser.
  • Your preferences (default view, theme, auto-enable) are stored locally on your device via chrome.storage.local and never leave it.
  • The extension does not make any network requests of its own.

Permissions

The extension requests access to web pages solely so it can detect and render JSON documents you open. It reads only the document you are currently viewing and modifies only how that document is displayed.
